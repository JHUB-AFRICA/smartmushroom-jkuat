JKUAT | JHUB Smart Mushroom pilot team

A local pilot built for farmers

Smart Mushroom Kenya is anchored at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT), where research, demonstration and farmer learning meet in one practical setting.

JHUB Africa supports innovation, digital onboarding and the connection between the growing room, the farmer dashboard and the people who will use the system.

Together, JKUAT, JHUB and the Smart Mushroom Kenya Pilot team are testing a system that farmers can understand, maintain and adapt.

Mush& adds expertise in mycelium biotechnology and sustainable food innovation, helping connect mushroom science with practical, future-ready applications.

The Smart Mushroom Kenya Pilot is funded by a grant from the European Union.
